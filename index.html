Early life

Zendaya was born on September 1, 1996, in Oakland, California, to teachers Claire Stoermer and Kazembe Ajamu Coleman. Her mother also worked as a house manager at the California Shakespeare Theater, allowing Zendaya to spend much of her childhood backstage learning the craft. Though she was initially so shy that she had to repeat kindergarten, she overcame her social anxiety by joining the hip-hop dance troupe Future Shock Oakland, studying hula dancing, and landing her first stage role at age six in a school play. She eventually turned this early exposure into formal training by enrolling at the Oakland School for the Arts, which laid the foundation for her later transition into professional child modeling and acting.

Career

Zendaya began her career as a child model and Disney Channel star on Shake It Up before successfully transitioning into a premier Hollywood A-lister. She achieved global box-office dominance starring in the Marvel Cinematic Universe Spider-Man franchise and the Dune films, while cementing her critical acclaim by becoming the youngest two-time Emmy winner for her raw performance in HBO's Euphoria. By 2026, she cemented her status as one of Hollywood's most bankable leading actors with projects like Challengers and Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey, all while remaining a definitive global fashion icon.

She also played MJ in the Marvel Spider-Man movies and appeared in the science-fiction film series Dune.

Awards

Zendaya has received several awards for her acting. Zendaya has accumulated over 37 major wins from nearly 120 award nominations Major Prestigious Awards • Primetime Emmy Awards: 2-time Winner (2020, 2022) for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Drama Series (Euphoria). At age 24, she became the youngest winner in the history of this category. She also achieved a nomination in 2026 for the series' final season. • Golden Globe Awards: 1-time Winner (2023) for Best Actress – Television Series Drama (Euphoria). She was also nominated in 2025 for Best Actress in a Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy for her lead performance in the film Challengers. • Screen Actors Guild (SAG) Awards: 1-time Nominee (2023) for Outstanding Performance by a Female Actor in a Drama Series (Euphoria). • Critics' Choice Movie/Television Awards: 3-time Winner from 7 nominations, with individual nominations spanning her work in Malcolm & Marie (2021) and Euphoria.

Personal life

Outside acting, Zendaya has an interest in fashion and music. She is also known for her appearances at major fashion events.Zendaya lives a relatively private life in Los Angeles, California, largely centering her personal narrative around her high-profile relationship with British actor Tom Holland, her Spider-Man co-star whom she has been dating since 2021. Raised by a tight-knit family of educators, she remains deeply connected to her large extended family, which includes five older half-siblings from her father’s previous marriage. Outside of her public persona as a global fashion icon and brand ambassador, Zendaya is an avid dog lover who owns a Miniature Schnauzer named Noon, and she utilizes her massive social media platform to champion philanthropic causes, focusing heavily on education, civil rights, and disaster relief.
